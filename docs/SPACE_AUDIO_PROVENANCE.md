## Authored musical specification

All six states are original sample-free compositions at 100 BPM, 4/4, centered on D. They use airy resonant FM plucks, softly detuned sine pads, clear sine/harmonic bass motion, occasional sine beacons and restrained dry electronic percussion. The signal family rises D–E toward A, with modal answers and breathing spaces.

- Menu, “A Beacon at the Edge of Dawn”: 16 bars / 38.4 seconds, looping. D-major/Lydian sixths and ninths, a B-minor reflection and homeward answer. Spacious offset lead entrances, 160 pitched events and 12 percussion events.
- Exploration, “Signals Beyond the Pale Ridge”: accepted 24-bar / 57.6-second D-major/Lydian pilot with a B-minor middle. 278 pitched events and 44 percussion events.
- Tension, “Motion Beneath the Silent Array”: 16 bars / 38.4 seconds, looping. D Dorian with E-flat/Phrygian inflections, D pedal, short interrupted calls, displaced bass/pluck accents. 248 pitched events and 120 percussion events.
- Combat, “Hold the Starfall Line”: accepted 24-bar / 57.6-second D-Dorian pilot with one F-sharp passing tone and eighth-grid propulsion. 428 pitched events and 294 percussion events.
- Victory, “The Outpost Answers the Stars”: four bars / 9.6 seconds, finite. Ascending D-major/Lydian signal, E6/G-sharp lift, D6/9 cadence and natural quiet tail. 36 pitched events and 17 percussion events.
- Defeat, “One Light Beyond the Debris”: five bars / 12 seconds, finite. Descending D-Aeolian signal, low final D/minor-ninth harmony and natural quiet tail. 25 pitched events and two percussion events.

Exact harmony, melody, note timing, dynamics, pan and percussion data are in the source and JSON scores. The MIDI programs approximate the custom voices; MIDI playback is not the mathematical master. `SPACE_SCORE_GUIDE.md` provides the complete bar-by-bar guide.

## Signal treatment and engineering lineage

The frozen core derives its utility conventions from the previously authored Hollow Lanterns infrastructure. Space musical tables, voices and reflection treatment are its own authored source. The extension reuses this exact core and adds finite, causal mixing/reflections for the result cues.

Loop voice releases, upper-instrument reflections and filter state are carried periodically. There is no seam-masking fade or post-hoc edge smoothing. Finite cues never wrap into their openings; every note release fits in its duration, and the tail decays naturally. Constant-gain calibration is followed by 16-bit TPDF dither, without limiting or clipping. Ogg uses Vorbis quality 5; MP3 uses 128 kbps with gapless metadata. Bitexact container metadata makes encoder outputs reproducible under the recorded toolchain. Quiet tails retain low-level dither and codec noise rather than literal digital silence.

No external samples, recordings, soundfonts, paid assets, music-generation services, artist imitation, credentials, external accounts or network synthesis were used. The tools are local Python, NumPy, SciPy, FFmpeg and Matplotlib. All toolchain versions are reported in the fresh validation.
