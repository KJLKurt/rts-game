## Remaining release and commercial gates

1. **Physical-device and uncoached usability.** Local automated/native desktop and emulated-touch checks pass. Test real Safari/iPhone, installed lifecycle and novice understanding without outside coaching.
2. **Performance at larger scale.** The historical 223.63ms cold movement tick remains a known synthetic gap. Profile final-source rendering/input on representative hardware; the 600 population ceiling is not a smooth-play guarantee.
3. **Natural content and balance coverage.** Outpost progression and the expedition first-battle defeat are verified; three preset-derived opening windows are documented. Exercise later natural campaign chapters and earned expedition branches, long/custom matches and broader difficulty/faction combinations.
4. **Subjective presentation and audio.** Browser geometry, material/crest images, research and credits passed. Dense-battle readability, complete listening and supplemental visual packs/frames remain separate work.
5. **Commercial asset clearance.** Publication authorization does not itself establish third-party/reference-art distribution rights. See [ASSETS.md](../ASSETS.md) and [ART_DIRECTION.md](ART_DIRECTION.md).
6. **Exact published release.** No new GitHub Actions or deployments were run for this cloud QA. Record the coordinated source commit, matching deployed assets, public update/Continue and hosted play before calling the published release verified.

## Conditional, future and supplemental scope

The original permits several biomes and a subset of modes now; eight biome examples, naval expansion, online multiplayer and cloud synchronization are future work. Formation/group systems and a manual graphics-quality selector are conditional. Named battle slots, an unrestricted visual scripting editor and full battlefield screen-reader navigation were not original mandatory features. A useful documented subset is permitted by section 53; broken shipped behavior is not.

Later discussions separately requested theme/style packs and fuller directional animation. The current candidate has one coherent sprite theme, four biome palettes, procedural motion and three attack strips. Those supplemental requests remain separately tracked in [COMMERCIAL_READINESS.md](COMMERCIAL_READINESS.md); this original-scope reconciliation neither implements nor cancels them. Do not expose incomplete packs as selectable finished content.

**Closure rule:** call a feature implemented when code supports it, locally checked when its relevant tests pass, and browser/play verified only when that exact candidate has evidence. Do not call the overall game complete or commercially ready while these release gates remain open.
