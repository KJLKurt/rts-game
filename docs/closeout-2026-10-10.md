## Backlog retained for a future decision

These are deferred review areas, not claims that every item is absent or broken:

- **Onboarding and controls:** clearer first-run guidance and usable phone-first interaction.
- **Buildings, actions and upgrades:** discoverability, selection, explanation and interaction acceptance.
- **Resources, production and placement:** economy feedback, queues, building placement and related usability.
- **HUD and preview layout:** corrected candidate browser acceptance, overlap checks and sequential-update behavior.
- **Maps and editor:** map usability plus editor pan and paint behavior.
- **Match length and pacing:** actual-play acceptance, balance and session-length review.
- **Art and real devices:** visual refinement and physical-device testing, including mobile acceptance.

Full original 56-section completion, broader commercial readiness and physical-device acceptance remain unclaimed.

## Resume only when requested

1. Confirm the desired scope with the owner and verify the current deployed runtime and repository head before touching source. A later documentation-only commit may advance the repository head without changing the deployed game.
2. Obtain the retained source and evidence archives from the owner and verify the checksums above.
3. Reconcile the current repository and QA refs before retrying the previously cancelled QA write. Preserve all original evidence and distinguish failures from later corrections.
4. Run the corrected browser/layout and sequential-update acceptance gates before considering candidate publication. Preserve the existing live release until acceptance passes.
5. Choose a bounded backlog slice and obtain any new authorization needed.

The development push is closed. No new feature, test or deployment cycle is part of this documentation handoff, and future development begins only if the owner asks to resume.
