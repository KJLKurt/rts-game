## Reopened priorities and next bounded batch

The owner priorities remain progressive learning, reliable controls, clear building purposes/actions/upgrades, visible income/queues/population, easier placement, collapsible HUD, configurable larger matches and editor pan/paint. Recent clarity batches improve those priorities; another feature-count expansion is not the next need.

Natural Ironwatch and subset-order screenshots show crowded bodies and health bars. Current actor rendering places selection and health feedback inside the depth-sorted loop, so foreground troops can cover it. Existing picking tests find an already exposed pixel before tapping; they do not establish human discoverability of a buried actor. The next candidate promotes restrained selected-individual/own-commander indicators without changing body depth, opacity, picking, orders or simulation spacing. Its own acceptance must record paired pixels, native exact-ID behavior, fog/death/respawn exclusions and bounded cost. No acceptance is implied by this audit alone.
